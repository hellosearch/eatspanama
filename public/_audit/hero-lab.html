Your second question - interlinking

Hub-and-spoke cross-links between occasions, neighborhoods & cuisines

Right now each occasion / neighborhood / cuisine page is an island. This module sits at the bottom of every one of them and links sideways to related pages - so a visitor (and Google) can travel Ceviche → Casco Viejo → Seafood without going back to the homepage. Below is how it would look on the Ceviche page:

Home / Panama City / Good for / Ceviche

Why it matters: these are reciprocal - the Casco Viejo page links back to "Ceviche," "Cocktail bars," etc. That is the hub-spoke interlinking in your SEO template rules (2026-07-14), and it is what turns a flat directory into something crawlable and browsable. It is independent of whichever hero you pick - it can ship alongside any of A/B/C.
